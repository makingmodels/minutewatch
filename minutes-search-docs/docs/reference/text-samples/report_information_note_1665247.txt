3.3    Public Sector Decarbonisation Scheme (PSDS)
3.4    Decarbonisation of Royston Leisure Centre was completed on 23 March 2026. The
       estimated completion date for the decarbonisation projects at Hitchin Swimming and
       Fitness Centre and North Herts Leisure Centre (Letchworth) has been delayed to April
       2027 due to ongoing delays in securing the necessary legal agreements with third-party
       landowners and UK Power Networks for new electricity supplies, substations, and
       connections to the UKPN High Voltage network.
3.5    One year since the new Waste service
3.6    We are now one year on from the waste service change, and the figures show the
       impressive difference that moving to three-weekly collections and providing additional
       recycling capacity have made for East and North Herts!
           •   36% reduction in non-recyclable waste over the first year across both districts
           •   325 tonnes of plastic bags and wrapping collected over both districts in the first
               year.
           •   19% increase in cardboard and paper recycling.
3.7    People’s Emergency Briefing
3.8    There have been screenings of the People's Emergency Briefing all over the district over
       the summer. The council has plans to put on a showing of the film to staff and councillors
       to improve understanding of the impacts and risks of the climate crisis. For residents
       looking to attend a screening of the briefing, go to the interactive screening map at:
       National Emergency Briefing.
3.9    Climate Engagement
3.10   The North Herts engagement hub for climate change and sustainability, the Climate
       Hive, now has over 1300 subscribers. We continue to keep residents up to date on the
       latest surveys and consultations, eco events, national environment days, the Cabinet
       Panel for the Environment, and council activity. Since the last panel, we have shared
       posts specifically surrounding the recent heatwaves. Among them have been
       information on staying safe and cool, reducing wildfire risk, sustainable cooling, and
       community cool spaces.
3.11   Local Plan consultation
3.12   The Local Plan team will be consulting on its Content and Evidence Base between 13
       October and 24 November 2026. Its purpose is to present the Council’s emerging
       policy approach across a range of thematic areas and set out what evidence has been
       or will be collected to support the New Local Plan | North Herts Council. Included in the
       consultation will be a map of the sites that have been submitted for consideration for
       site allocations for the new draft Local Plan.
3.13   Green Space consultation
3.14   The Greenspace team want to hear from residents about our parks and green spaces.
       They are consulting on which green spaces are used, and what’s important to users in
       order to preserve and improve facilities. Feedback will inform the next Greenspace
       Management Strategy thd future service design (previous strategy here). The survey
       will close on the 28 September – participate digitally in the consultation here. Find out
       more information and how you can have your say in person at Baldock, Royston or
       Letchworth Community Forums in this article.
3.15   Water Preservation in our Allotments
3.16   The council’s Greenspace team are exploring a project to reduce the use of water at
       allotments through water harvesting and recycling. This would allow allotments to
       adapt better to the changing climate and save water!
3.17   Community Energy

       involved. The page gives information and contact details about local community energy
       groups and shows those interested how they can get involved.

4   SUSTAINABILITY STRATEGY 2025-2030 UPDATES
4.1    The Sustainability Strategy 2025-2030 is available here: Sustainability strategy | North
       Herts Council. Actions are being monitored by our Climate and Sustainability Officer
       Group and Political Liaison Board which is comprised of Cabinet Members and our
       Leadership Team.
       Key progress made so far includes:
           •   An EV salary sacrifice scheme is now in place to help staff invest in lower
               emission vehicles and reduce staff commuting emissions. This has generated
               quite a lot of interest with a high number of registrations. A staff commuting
               survey was launched in July to understand staff commuting behaviours and
               identify possible actions that can be taken.
           •   A mandatory green procurement checklist is in place, and we have been
               working through multiple procurements to use the checklist to identify
               proportional ways to embed sustainability into NHC projects. Further work
               ongoing to identify other ways to implement sustainability further into the
               procurement process.
           •   We continue to deliver the government funded Warm Homes Local Grant
               scheme aimed at supporting homes in fuel poverty in North Herts with
               insulation, solar panels and clean heating. For year 1, we delivered installations
               at 12 homes. In year 2, we are targeting installations in up to 30 homes.
           •   The North Herts Design Code SPD has been launched on the NHC website and
               incorporates biodiversity, climate adaptation and low emissions travel.
           •   Internal communications around sustainability continue to be promoted, to help
               inform NHC staff about climate change and their role within it, as a Council
               officer and in their personal lives. We continue to promote carbon literacy
               training and the internal sustainability module, which sits at 93% completion as
               of June 2026.
           •   The new waste service has now been in place for one year, and the waste team
               continue to engage with residents and implement new waste campaigns to
               increase resident participation and increase recycling rates.

       5.2 will only be placed on the work programme if agreed by the Chair or at a meeting of
       the Panel.
5.4    Attendance from external bodies and members of the public is actively encouraged.
5.5    This is a non-decision-making advisory body and therefore this or any notes/reports to
       the Panel do not include a recommendation on a key Executive decision and have not

       mind for any recommendations of the Panel.
