                              North Yorkshire Council
                     Statutory Licensing Sub-Committee
Minutes of the meeting held on Wednesday, 16 September 2026 commencing at 10.00 am.
Councillors Derek Bastiman, Subash Sharma and Greg White.
Officers present: Jane Graham – Democratic Services Officer, Sarah Owen – Solicitor, Guy
Stephenson – Environmental Protection Officer and John Wardell – Senior Licensing Enforcement
Officer
Other Attendees: 6 members of the public.
                 Copies of all documents considered are in the Minute Book
1      Election of Chair
       That Councillor Greg White be elected as Chair for the duration of the meeting.
       (Councillor White in the Chair)
2      Apologies for absence
       There were no apologies for absence.
3      Disclosures of interest
       For the purpose of transparency Councillor Greg White declared he was a personal
       licence holder and a fellow of the British Institute of Innkeeping.
4      Procedure for meeting
       Decision
       The Sub-Committee agreed the procedure for the meeting as outlined by the Chair.
5      Application to vary a Premises Licence - Fusion, 94-100 St Thomas Street,
       Scarborough, YO11 1DU
       The Corporate Director – Environment sought the determination by the Sub-Committee of
       an application for the variation of a premises licence for Fusion, 94-100 St Thomas Street,
       Scarborough YO11 1DU.
       The options available to the Sub-Committee were:
           •   To grant the licence as applied for,
           •   To grant the licence with amended and/or modified conditions: or
           •   To reject the whole or part of the application.
       Decision
       That the hearing be adjourned so that an acoustic assessment could be provided.
The meeting concluded at 11.06 am.
